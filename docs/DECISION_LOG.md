| D-062 | 2026-11-26 | **Day closing and expenses as built (Phase 7):** one **cash day** (`cash_sessions`) per business date, opened with the counted float and closed with the counted drawer; expected cash = float + cash in − cash out over the day's payments and cash movements. **Every payment belongs to exactly one cash day**: a payment made while a day is open is stamped with it inside the payment's own transaction (the day's row is locked, so a closing at the same instant waits or finishes first — a bill can never fall between two days); a payment made while no day is open is **adopted by the next day that opens or reopens**, because that is the drawer the money is in.
